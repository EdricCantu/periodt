offset calibration

Set the time for the next bell is expected to ring.

Press the ⏺️ button to begin recording.
The number changing rapidly represents the offset from the time the bell should ring, and now.

When a bell sound is detected from the microphone, the number will freeze instantly and turn yellow.
If the bell persists for ~5 seconds, it is a period changing bell. The offset will turn green.
If not, the offset will turn red for one second, then black, and unfreeze as if nothing happened.

The offset, once green, represents the offset of the bell system's clock from real time. Recording will halt.

If needed, after pressing the ⏺️ button, press the Calibrate Calibrator button once audibly, or in a quiet room.
It determines the delay between the occurence of sound and the javascript interpreter receiving it,
since touch events are near instant compared to mic processing.
